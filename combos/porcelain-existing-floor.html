Porcelain on Existing tile (tile-over-tile) – Floor

Thinset / mortar

Modified ANSI A118.4 or improved modified A118.15 mortar (e.g., LATICRETE 254 Platinum exceeds A118.4/A118.11/A118.15).

Sources: ANSI A108/A118/A136.1-2020 preview (TCNA/ANSI) LATICRETE – 254 Platinum data sheet

Trowel notch & coverage

CUSTOM chart: 1/4×1/4×1/4 in square notch for tile up to 8 in; 1/4×3/8×1/4 in square notch for 8–16 in. Confirm coverage by pulling tiles.

Sources: CUSTOM – Set Tools / trowel chart LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage

Coverage: Min 80% average contact; corners and edges supported.

Sources: LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage CUSTOM – VersaBond-LHT

Membrane options

Membrane over existing tile: check membrane data sheet for existing-tile substrate and primer/skim-coat requirements. verify with manufacturer

  • CUSTOM RedGard (liquid): Meets ANSI A118.10 and A118.12 high performance (cracks to 1/8 in); IAPMO shower pan liner; 0.35 perms at steam spec (<0.5 required); steam showers applied 40–45 mils per TB40 (follow TCNA SR614/SR615).
    Sources: CUSTOM – RedGard TDS-104 CUSTOM – RedGard steam shower TB40
  • LATICRETE HYDRO BAN family: Meets/exceeds A118.10 and A118.12; tile over with A118.4, A118.15 or A118.3 adhesive. LATICRETE lists HYDRO BAN, HYDRO BAN Sheet Membrane, Quick Cure and 9235 for steam rooms with compatible LATICRETE thin-sets.
    Sources: LATICRETE – Waterproofing membranes LATICRETE – Steam Room Technical Design Manual

Key TCNA / ANSI notes

  • Existing tile must be sound, well bonded and cleaned/scarified; mortar must list existing ceramic tile as a substrate. The Ceramic Tile Foundation advises against tile-over-tile in continuous-use steam showers (existing vapor barrier can't be verified; second membrane may trap moisture) — full tear-out recommended.
    Sources: Ceramic Tile Foundation – Tile Over It or Take It Up? LATICRETE – Glass Tile Adhesive Lite
