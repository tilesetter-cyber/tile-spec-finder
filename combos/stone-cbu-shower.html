Natural stone on Cement board – Shower / wet area

Thinset / mortar

ANSI A118.4/A118.15 mortar; white mortar for light marble. Moisture-sensitive stone (e.g., green marble) may require special setting materials such as epoxy — ask stone supplier. verify with manufacturer

If using a Schluter membrane: tile goes over it with Schluter SET/ALL-SET/FAST-SET or unmodified A118.1 (see membrane card).

Sources: ANSI A108/A118/A136.1-2020 preview (TCNA/ANSI) Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA-HEAT

Trowel notch & coverage

Ungauged stone may require larger notch sizes and flat back-troweling (CUSTOM).

Sources: CUSTOM – VersaBond-LHT

Coverage: 95% average contact, corners and edges fully supported (ANSI A108.5).

Sources: LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage CUSTOM – VersaBond-LHT

Membrane options

Bonded waterproofing membrane meeting ANSI A118.10 required — mortars are not waterproofing.

Sources: LATICRETE – Waterproofing membranes TCNA – ANSI Standards list
  • Schluter KERDI (8 mil sheet waterproofing): Bond with Schluter SET/ALL-SET/FAST-SET or unmodified thin-set; seams 2 in overlap or KERDI-BAND. Suitable for showers and intermittent-use steam showers (0.90 perms).
    Sources: Schluter – KERDI product page Schluter – Shower System Installation Handbook (PDF) Schluter FAQ – KERDI in steam showers
  • CUSTOM RedGard (liquid): Meets ANSI A118.10 and A118.12 high performance (cracks to 1/8 in); IAPMO shower pan liner; 0.35 perms at steam spec (<0.5 required); steam showers applied 40–45 mils per TB40 (follow TCNA SR614/SR615).
    Sources: CUSTOM – RedGard TDS-104 CUSTOM – RedGard steam shower TB40
  • MAPEI Mapelastic AquaDefense (liquid): Exceeds A118.10-LV and A118.12; IAPMO shower-pan liner; 0.35 perms. MAPEI's installation guide states a vapor barrier is required with it in steam rooms; not for OSB.
    Sources: MAPEI – Mapelastic AquaDefense TDS MAPEI – AquaDefense installation guide
  • LATICRETE HYDRO BAN family: Meets/exceeds A118.10 and A118.12; tile over with A118.4, A118.15 or A118.3 adhesive. LATICRETE lists HYDRO BAN, HYDRO BAN Sheet Membrane, Quick Cure and 9235 for steam rooms with compatible LATICRETE thin-sets.
    Sources: LATICRETE – Waterproofing membranes LATICRETE – Steam Room Technical Design Manual

Key TCNA / ANSI notes

  • Cement board is not waterproof — add a bonded A118.10 membrane in wet areas.
    Sources: LATICRETE – Waterproofing membranes
  • Deflection: L/720 for natural stone over wood framing (TCNA assemblies / MIA) vs L/360 for ceramic. Stone over wood with backer board: TCNA F250 — 19/32 in T&G ply subfloor + 15/32 in ply underlayment (residential), joists max 16 in o.c.
    Sources: Boise Cascade GE-06 – TCNA 2023 wood-framed floor assemblies table Stone World – Natural stone over wood framing (F250, L/720) SBCA – Special floor loading report (ANSI A108 L/360; MIA L/720)
