Ceramic (glazed wall/floor) on Drywall (gypsum board) – Wall (dry)

Thinset / mortar

ANSI A118.4 modified mortar generally. Use of A118.1 dry-set for interior wall tile not confirmed from a source here. verify with manufacturer

Sources: ANSI A108/A118/A136.1-2020 preview (TCNA/ANSI)

Trowel notch & coverage

CUSTOM chart: 1/4×1/4×1/4 in square notch up to 8 in; 1/4×3/8×1/4 in square notch 8–16 in.

Sources: CUSTOM – Set Tools / trowel chart

Coverage: Min 80% average contact (ANSI A108.5).

Sources: LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage

Membrane options

None required in dry areas.

Key TCNA / ANSI notes

  • Dry interior walls only.
    Sources: LATICRETE – Glass Tile Adhesive Lite
