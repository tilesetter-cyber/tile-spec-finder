Porcelain on Concrete slab – Exterior

Thinset / mortar

Modified ANSI A118.4 or improved modified A118.15 mortar (e.g., LATICRETE 254 Platinum exceeds A118.4/A118.11/A118.15).

If using a Schluter membrane: tile goes over it with Schluter SET/ALL-SET/FAST-SET or unmodified A118.1 (see membrane card).

Sources: ANSI A108/A118/A136.1-2020 preview (TCNA/ANSI) LATICRETE – 254 Platinum data sheet Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA

Trowel notch & coverage

CUSTOM chart: 1/4×1/4×1/4 in square notch for tile up to 8 in; 1/4×3/8×1/4 in square notch for 8–16 in. Confirm coverage by pulling tiles.

Sources: CUSTOM – Set Tools / trowel chart LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage

Coverage: 95% average contact, edges supported (ANSI A108.5).

Sources: LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage MAPEI – LHT Mortars

Membrane options

Tile must be manufacturer-rated for exterior/freeze-thaw (ASTM C1026); provide drainage — standing water and ice in joints damage even frost-resistant tile. Waterproofing over occupied space; AquaDefense is not for use as primary membrane over occupied space.

Sources: TCNA FAQ – Frost resistance TCNA – Ceramic Tile in an Exterior Installation MAPEI – AquaDefense installation guide

Key TCNA / ANSI notes

  • Honor movement joints through tile. Crack isolation membranes per ANSI A118.12 / installed per A108.17.
    Sources: TCNA – ANSI Standards list
  • Freeze-thaw: Porcelain is ≤0.5% absorption (impervious) but only the manufacturer can rate a tile for exterior; look for ASTM C1026 freeze-thaw results.
    Sources: TCNA – Ceramic Tile in an Exterior Installation TCNA FAQ – Frost resistance
