Large format / gauged porcelain panels on Concrete slab – Floor

Thinset / mortar

Tile with any side >15 in or >5 lb/ft²: LHT mortar meeting A118.4H or A118.15H (bond coat 3/32–1/2 in after embedding). Gauged porcelain tile panels/slabs: ANSI A108.19 (interior) / A108.20 (exterior), mortar per panel maker.

If using a Schluter membrane: tile goes over it with Schluter SET/ALL-SET/FAST-SET or unmodified A118.1 (see membrane card).

Sources: TCNA Spotlight – Handbook changes (LHT mortar) MAPEI – LHT Mortars TCNA – ANSI Standards list LATICRETE – 254 Platinum data sheet Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA

Trowel notch & coverage

Ridge-collapsing notch (U, slant, LFT), e.g., 3/4×9/16×3/8 in U-notch at 30° (CUSTOM); CUSTOM advises against 1/2×1/2 in square; back-trowel as needed. Panel-specific trowel/back-butter: per panel maker.

Sources: CUSTOM – VersaBond-LHT LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage

Coverage: Min 80% average contact; corners and edges supported.

Sources: LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage CUSTOM – VersaBond-LHT

Membrane options

Crack isolation (A118.12) over shrinkage cracks; DITRA bonded with unmodified A118.1 over concrete.

  • CUSTOM RedGard (liquid): Meets ANSI A118.10 and A118.12 high performance (cracks to 1/8 in); IAPMO shower pan liner; 0.35 perms at steam spec (<0.5 required); steam showers applied 40–45 mils per TB40 (follow TCNA SR614/SR615).
    Sources: CUSTOM – RedGard TDS-104 CUSTOM – RedGard steam shower TB40
  • MAPEI Mapelastic AquaDefense (liquid): Exceeds A118.10-LV and A118.12; IAPMO shower-pan liner; 0.35 perms. MAPEI's installation guide states a vapor barrier is required with it in steam rooms; not for OSB.
    Sources: MAPEI – Mapelastic AquaDefense TDS MAPEI – AquaDefense installation guide
  • LATICRETE HYDRO BAN family: Meets/exceeds A118.10 and A118.12; tile over with A118.4, A118.15 or A118.3 adhesive. LATICRETE lists HYDRO BAN, HYDRO BAN Sheet Membrane, Quick Cure and 9235 for steam rooms with compatible LATICRETE thin-sets.
    Sources: LATICRETE – Waterproofing membranes LATICRETE – Steam Room Technical Design Manual
  • Schluter DITRA / DITRA-XL (uncoupling): Bond DITRA with ANSI A118.11 modified thin-set over plywood/OSB; unmodified A118.1 over concrete or gypsum. Tile over DITRA: Schluter SET, ALL-SET, FAST-SET, or unmodified A118.1 (Schluter advises against other brands' modified mortars here due to slow cure).
    Sources: Schluter FAQ – thin-set to install DITRA Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA Schluter – DITRA Installation Handbook (PDF)

Key TCNA / ANSI notes

  • Honor movement joints through tile. Crack isolation membranes per ANSI A118.12 / installed per A108.17.
    Sources: TCNA – ANSI Standards list
  • TCNA: LHT mortar cannot be used to level or flatten substrates or build slopes.
    Sources: TCNA Spotlight – Handbook changes (LHT mortar)
