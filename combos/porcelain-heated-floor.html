Porcelain on Heated floor – Floor

Thinset / mortar

Modified ANSI A118.4 or improved modified A118.15 mortar (e.g., LATICRETE 254 Platinum exceeds A118.4/A118.11/A118.15).

If using a Schluter membrane: tile goes over it with Schluter SET/ALL-SET/FAST-SET or unmodified A118.1 (see membrane card).

Sources: ANSI A108/A118/A136.1-2020 preview (TCNA/ANSI) LATICRETE – 254 Platinum data sheet Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA

Trowel notch & coverage

CUSTOM chart: 1/4×1/4×1/4 in square notch for tile up to 8 in; 1/4×3/8×1/4 in square notch for 8–16 in. Confirm coverage by pulling tiles.

Sources: CUSTOM – Set Tools / trowel chart LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage

Coverage: Min 80% average contact; corners and edges supported.

Sources: LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage CUSTOM – VersaBond-LHT

Membrane options

  • Schluter DITRA-HEAT (uncoupling + heating cable): Tile over DITRA-HEAT: Schluter SET, ALL-SET, FAST-SET, or unmodified A118.1. Moisture-sensitive stone (e.g., green marble) may need special setting materials.
    Sources: Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA-HEAT Schluter – DITRA Installation Handbook (PDF)
  • Schluter DITRA / DITRA-XL (uncoupling): Bond DITRA with ANSI A118.11 modified thin-set over plywood/OSB; unmodified A118.1 over concrete or gypsum. Tile over DITRA: Schluter SET, ALL-SET, FAST-SET, or unmodified A118.1 (Schluter advises against other brands' modified mortars here due to slow cure).
    Sources: Schluter FAQ – thin-set to install DITRA Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA Schluter – DITRA Installation Handbook (PDF)

Key TCNA / ANSI notes

  • Install cables per heat maker; with DITRA, cables are encapsulated in ALL-SET/FAST-SET/modified thin-set or leveler, cured, then DITRA set with SET or unmodified mortar. DITRA-HEAT holds cables without leveler.
    Sources: Schluter – DITRA Installation Handbook (PDF)
