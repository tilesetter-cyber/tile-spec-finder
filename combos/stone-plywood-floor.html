Natural stone on Plywood subfloor – Floor

Thinset / mortar

ANSI A118.4/A118.15 mortar; white mortar for light marble. Moisture-sensitive stone (e.g., green marble) may require special setting materials such as epoxy — ask stone supplier. verify with manufacturer

Mortar must be rated for exterior-glue plywood (A118.11).

If using a Schluter membrane: tile goes over it with Schluter SET/ALL-SET/FAST-SET or unmodified A118.1 (see membrane card).

Sources: ANSI A108/A118/A136.1-2020 preview (TCNA/ANSI) Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA-HEAT Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA

Trowel notch & coverage

Ungauged stone may require larger notch sizes and flat back-troweling (CUSTOM).

Sources: CUSTOM – VersaBond-LHT

Coverage: Min 80% average contact; corners and edges supported.

Sources: LATICRETE TDS 196 – Trowel selection & coverage CUSTOM – VersaBond-LHT

Membrane options

Uncoupling membrane (DITRA bonded with A118.11 modified over plywood) or cement backer per the chosen TCNA method.

  • Schluter DITRA / DITRA-XL (uncoupling): Bond DITRA with ANSI A118.11 modified thin-set over plywood/OSB; unmodified A118.1 over concrete or gypsum. Tile over DITRA: Schluter SET, ALL-SET, FAST-SET, or unmodified A118.1 (Schluter advises against other brands' modified mortars here due to slow cure).
    Sources: Schluter FAQ – thin-set to install DITRA Schluter FAQ – thin-set for tile over DITRA Schluter – DITRA Installation Handbook (PDF)

Key TCNA / ANSI notes

  • TCNA 2023 wood floor assemblies (per Boise Cascade summary): most limit joists to 16 in o.c. (3 allow 19.2 in, 6 allow 24 in). Examples: F148 — 23/32 in ext-glue T&G ply + uncoupling membrane at 19.2 in o.c.; F147 — 23/32 in ply + 3/8 in ply underlayment at 24 in o.c. Deflection L/360 ceramic, L/720 stone. Exterior-glue plywood only; not over particle board, luan or interior-glue ply.
    Sources: Boise Cascade GE-06 – TCNA 2023 wood-framed floor assemblies table APA TT-006 – Ceramic Tile Over Wood Structural Panel Floors LATICRETE – Glass Tile Adhesive data sheet
  • Deflection: L/720 for natural stone over wood framing (TCNA assemblies / MIA) vs L/360 for ceramic. Stone over wood with backer board: TCNA F250 — 19/32 in T&G ply subfloor + 15/32 in ply underlayment (residential), joists max 16 in o.c.
    Sources: Boise Cascade GE-06 – TCNA 2023 wood-framed floor assemblies table Stone World – Natural stone over wood framing (F250, L/720) SBCA – Special floor loading report (ANSI A108 L/360; MIA L/720)
